# DNGWORLD content architecture

**Positioning:** Business Growth Intelligence. DNGWORLD helps businesses identify constraints, prioritise decisions and align Strategy, Marketing, Media, Data, AI and Automation into a coherent growth system. Not an advertising-only agency.

## Page narrative
1. Problem: costly traffic, weak conversion, fractured data and collaboration.
2. Value: clarify constraints before spending; integrate execution and insight.
3. Solutions: six capability layers, separate but connected.
4. Evidence: public tools (`/tools/`), analysis (`/insights/`) and deliverables (`/services/`); no fabricated case-study outcomes.
5. Action: assess the business problem and contact DNGWORLD.

## Six capability definitions
- Strategy: market/customer diagnostics, positioning, growth roadmap.
- Marketing: acquisition, performance, funnel/CRO, sales alignment.
- Media: content architecture, creative, brand narratives, video.
- Data: tracking, dashboards, attribution, decision support.
- AI: knowledge, analytical assistance, content support with human controls.
- Automation: CRM, lifecycle, workflow integration, operational efficiency.

## Naming language
Site brand: **DNGWORLD** (not DNGWORKS). Descriptor: **Business Growth Intelligence**. English and Vietnamese headings may coexist by V24 art direction. Avoid unsupported KPI/outcome claims.
